1. OpenXR SDK headers
---------------------

The native part (dxgi.dll) was compiled with the OpenXR SDK headers
(openxr.h, openxr_platform.h, openxr_platform_defines.h,
openxr_loader_negotiation.h), version release-1.1.63.
No OpenXR loader binary is shipped with this mod; it talks to the OpenXR
runtime (for example SteamVR) that is installed on your PC.

2. Teardown
-----------

Teardown, its game files, tool models, textures, sounds and all other
assets are the property of Tuxedo Labs. None of them are included in this
release or in the Steam Workshop item.

The mod shows a copy of the held tool by loading the tool models
(data\vox\tool\*.vox) directly from YOUR OWN Teardown installation
while the game runs. These files are never copied or redistributed.

The Teardown VR Mod is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated
with, endorsed by or supported by Tuxedo Labs.
